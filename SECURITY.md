# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability in BabyBlocks or any of our apps, email security@babyblocks.io with the details. We aim to acknowledge within 2 business days.

Please include:
- A description of the issue and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce, including any proof-of-concept code
- Your contact information (and whether you'd like to be credited)

## What we ask

- Give us a reasonable window to investigate and patch before disclosing publicly
- Do not access or modify user data beyond what's necessary to demonstrate the issue
- Do not run automated scans against our production infrastructure without prior coordination

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who follow this policy in good faith.

## Scope

In scope:
- BudgetBaby, PantryBaby, BabyBlocks CRM iOS apps
- babyblocks.io, support.babyblocks.io, admin.babyblocks.io
- bb-* Cloudflare Workers under timothy-9d8.workers.dev and api.babyblocks.io
- Firebase projects for BabyBlocks

Out of scope:
- Denial-of-service attacks
- Reports from automated scanners without manual validation
- Issues in third-party services (Microsoft, Apple, Google) — please report directly to them
- Social engineering of operators or customers

## No bug bounty (yet)

We do not currently run a paid bug bounty program. We do offer recognition (credit in this file, with your permission) for valid findings.
